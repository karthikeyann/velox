Verification Notes

  • velox/connectors/ has exactly 4 top-level connector subdirectories: fuzzer/, hive/, tpch/, tpcds/ (plus tests/ which is not a connector). Confirmed via filesystem listing.
  • Under hive/, two sub-connectors exist: iceberg/ and paimon/. Each has its own ConnectorFactory and a distinct registered name. storage_adapters/ and benchmarks/ are not connectors.
  • velox/experimental/cudf/connectors/ contains exactly one subdirectory: hive/. Its CMakeLists.txt has only add_subdirectory(hive).
  • CudfHiveConnector (line 32 of CudfHiveConnector.h) extends HiveConnector, and CudfHiveConnectorFactory extends HiveConnectorFactory.
  • No other cuDF connector implementations exist anywhere in velox/experimental/cudf/.
  • hive/storage_adapters/ provides filesystem I/O plugins (S3, HDFS, GCS, ABFS) — these are not separate connectors in the Connector/ConnectorFactory sense.

Key Observations

  • Only 1 out of 6 Velox core connectors has a cuDF GPU counterpart today.
  • Since Iceberg and Paimon both extend HiveConnector, a cuDF implementation for those could potentially build on CudfHiveConnector — but that work does not exist yet.
  • TPC-H, TPC-DS, and Fuzzer are synthetic / in-memory data generators where GPU acceleration at the connector level is less relevant (GPU acceleration matters more at the execution/expression layer for these workloads).
  • Considering only the 3 production-grade I/O connectors (Hive, Iceberg, Paimon), the cuDF coverage is 1 out of 3 (33.3%).
